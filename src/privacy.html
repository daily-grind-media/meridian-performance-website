Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Meridian Performance respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Meridian Performance collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, submit a form, schedule an appointment, communicate with us, or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, information submitted through website forms, appointment or consultation information, and other information you choose to provide when communicating with Meridian Performance.

We may also collect basic technical information associated with use of our website, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and similar website usage information.

How We Use Your Information

Information collected by Meridian Performance may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Schedule and manage consultations or appointments
  • Provide information about Meridian Performance services
  • Communicate with current and prospective clients
  • Send service-related communications
  • Send marketing communications when you have provided the appropriate consent
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Maintain records and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

SMS and Mobile Messaging

If you provide your mobile phone number and separately consent to receive text messages from Meridian Performance, we may send SMS messages related to the categories of communication for which you provided consent.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers

Meridian Performance may use service providers to support business operations, such as website hosting, customer relationship management, appointment scheduling, communications, analytics, and other operational services.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services on behalf of Meridian Performance and are not authorized to use SMS opt-in data or consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain personal information, although doing so may limit our ability to provide certain services or respond to requests.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe instructions contained in those communications.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Children’s Privacy

Meridian Performance’s website and services are not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
